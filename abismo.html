abismo

Proyect
Fashion Design
Year
2026
Client
University Project
Areas
Fashion Design · Material Research · Art Direction

ABISMO is a conceptual fashion project inspired by the phenomenon of bioluminescence found in deep-sea organisms. Through the use of fluorescent biomaterials and organic silhouettes, the collection explores themes of transformation, communication, and female empowerment. The project translates natural processes into a contemporary visual language that connects science, material experimentation, and fashion design.
