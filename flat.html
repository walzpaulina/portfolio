FLAT

Proyect
Product Design
Year
2026
Client
University Project
Areas
Industrial Design · Product Design · Packaging Design

FLAT is a flat-pack stool designed around the concept of optimization. The project explores how reducing the number of components can simplify manufacturing, transportation, storage, and assembly while maintaining a strong visual identity. Combining folded steel and OSB panels, FLAT offers an efficient and intuitive product system inspired by contemporary modular furniture design.
