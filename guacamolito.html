Guacamolito

Proyect
Brand Identity & Packaging
Year
2025
Client
Martina Walz (proyecto de tesis)
Areas
Brand Identity · Packaging Design · Art Direction

Guacamolito is a branding and packaging project developed for a dehydrated guacamole product. The visual identity was designed to communicate freshness, simplicity, and accessibility through organic graphic elements, a vibrant color palette, and a cohesive packaging system. The result is a flexible and distinctive brand experience that stands out within the food category.
