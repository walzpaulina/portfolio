MÓDULO TEU

Proyect
Urban Design
Year
2026
Client
University Project
Areas
Urban Design · Industrial Design · 3D Visualization

Módulo TEU is a bus shelter proposal designed for Puerto Madero, Buenos Aires. Developed from repurposed shipping containers, the project investigates how existing industrial structures can be transformed into functional public infrastructure. The design combines durability, sustainability, and user comfort through integrated lighting, weather protection, and a clear architectural identity.
